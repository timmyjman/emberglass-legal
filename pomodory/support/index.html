How do I restore my purchases?

Open Pomodory, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel Pomodory Plus?

Open your iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Pomodory. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Lifetime is a one-time purchase and never renews.

Why did my egg go cold?

An egg stays warm while you stay in the app. If you leave for more than a few seconds during a session, it goes cold. A Warming Stone from the shop gives you longer to come back.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check that notifications are on for Pomodory in your iPhone's Settings, and that Break Is Over and Streak Reminder are on in the app's Settings.

Will I lose my wobbles if I get a new phone?

Your progress is saved on your phone, and it can be included in your iPhone or iCloud backup. Restoring from that backup brings it back. Purchases can always be restored with Restore Purchases.
