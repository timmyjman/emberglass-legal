Pomodory

Privacy Policy

For Pomodory: Focus Timer on iPhone. Effective November 24, 2026.

The short version

  • Pomodory has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your eggs, wobbles, blanket, streak, pips and settings stay on your phone.
  • Purchases go through Apple. We use one service, RevenueCat, to check what you have bought.
  • We do not sell or share your information.

Who we are

Pomodory: Focus Timer is made by Tim Headrick, publishing as Emberglass Software. In this policy, "we" means the developer and "you" means anyone using the app.

What stays on your phone

Everything you do in Pomodory is saved only on your device: your focus sessions, the eggs you warm, the wobbles you hatch, your blanket patches and streak, your pips and shop items, and your settings. We have no servers that receive this, so we cannot see it.

If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it. It may be included in your own iPhone or iCloud backups, which Apple handles under your device settings.

Purchases and subscriptions

Pomodory Plus and in-app items are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes your payment; we never see your name, Apple ID, card or billing details.

To know which purchases and subscriptions are active, the app uses RevenueCat. When you open the app, buy something or restore purchases, RevenueCat receives:

  • a random identifier the app creates, which is not linked to your name or email;
  • your purchase and subscription history for Pomodory;
  • basic technical details, such as device model, iOS version, app version, country or region, and IP address.

This is used only to unlock what you paid for. Apple's handling of App Store purchases is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

Reminders like "The egg is getting cold" and "Break's over" are scheduled on your phone by the app. Nothing about them is sent to us. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in your iPhone's Settings.

What we don't use

Pomodory does not use your microphone, camera, photos, contacts or location. Sounds and vibrations only play; nothing is recorded. The app does not track you across other apps or websites, does not show ads, and does not sell or share your information with anyone.

Children

Pomodory does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The only data that leaves the device is the purchase information described above, which Apple and RevenueCat need to provide in-app purchases.

Your choices

  • Delete the app to delete all of your Pomodory data.
  • Use Restore Purchases in the app to bring back what you bought on a new phone.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription in your iPhone's Settings, under your name, then Subscriptions.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date at the top.
