Pomodory

A gentle focus timer. Warm an egg while you focus, hatch a wobble when the timer ends, and help Woolly stitch his blanket one day at a time.

Download on the App Store

Focus, then hatch

Pick an egg and start a session. Stay with it and a little wobble hatches at the end.

Kind when you slip

Leave the app and the egg cools. It is still yours, so come back and keep warming.

No ads, no account

Nothing to sign up for, and the app collects no personal data.
